Midad storefront review: HomeView.vue, CategoryView.vue, ProductView.vue.

Overall: a distinctive Arabic perfume identity, visually around 8/10; provisional Nielsen score 28/40. Strong warm ink/ivory/gold palette, Arabic type hierarchy, discovery sampling proposition and reassuring purchase details. The opportunity is faster scent comparison and clearer mobile buying.

Priority issues:
1. P1: Shared bottle photos dominate product cards while scent differences remain small text. Keep packaging consistent and promote scent family/signature with truthful distinctive imagery or restrained ingredient accents.
2. P1: Cards display lowest price but plus adds 50 ml. Expose size and its actual price, or open a size selector.
3. P2: Mobile PDP places large gallery and thumbnails before identity/price; initial viewport does not reach purchase choices. Move compact name/signature/price above image or reduce gallery footprint.
4. P2: Repeated torn edges, paper frames, gold tracing and ingredient ornament weaken hierarchy. Keep signature flowing bands, simplify selected scenes and routine shopping surfaces.
5. P2: Up to 14 note chips ask novices to understand ingredients before choosing. Lead with familiar scent families and disclose note filters later.

Minor: Generic hero shop CTA routes to men. Inspired-by and signature text deserves more prominence. Illustrated Attar visual register differs from photography. Do not treat unloaded lazy images in old fullpage screenshots as proven defects.

Personas: mobile social visitor reaches identity/price late; first-time buyer struggles to compare similar cards and technical notes; gift buyer needs truthful exact-item/packaging evidence.

Cognitive load moderate: long note rail, repeated imagery and ornamental density. Emotional journey strong at hero/discovery, repetitive through featured products, helpful adviser and gift finish.

Nielsen (0-4): system status 3, real-world match 3, control 3, consistency 3, prevention 2, recognition 2, efficiency 3, aesthetics 3, recovery 3, help 3. Total 28/40. Provisional; no completed checkout test.

Recommended first pass: clearer product cards and purchase sizes/prices, mobile PDP hierarchy, then reduce repeated ornament while preserving identity.
